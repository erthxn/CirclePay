 Card studio

    
Pick your line style and theme.

    
Arc is the CirclePay payment card: money flows along the arcs, coin chips tick up as each payment lands, and a receipt strip holds your handle, the date and the circle total. Arc is always yellow on black. Spiral, Solid and the other line styles take the theme you pick. Every agent can draw the same Arc card from the open Arc card spec (MIT).

 How CirclePay works

    
From handle to circle card in four moves.

    

      

        01
        
Drop your X handle

        
Just type it, and CirclePay takes it from there.

        

      

      

        02
        
It reads the last 7 days

        
Who replied, quoted and reposted you. Public activity only.

        

      

      

        03
        
It builds your circle card

        
Curved arcs out to the people who showed up, an amount pill under each, your lowercase handle and the date.

        

          
        

      

      

        04
        
Optionally reward the circle through PayBox

        
Only after your clear yes. Nothing goes out on its own, and you confirm every payout.
