How CirclePay works

    
From handle to circle card in four moves.

    

      

        1 · Handle
        
Drop your X handle

        
Just type it, and CirclePay takes it from there.

        

      

      

        2 · Read
        
It reads the last 7 days

        
Who replied, quoted and reposted you. Public activity only.

        

      

      

        3 · Card
        
It builds your circle card

        
Curved arcs out to the people who showed up, an amount pill under each, your lowercase handle and the date.

        

          
        

      

      

        4 · Reward
        
Optionally reward the circle through PayBox

        
Only after your clear yes. Nothing goes out on its own, and you confirm every payout.

Connect your agent

    
Pick a path. Pay only when you want to.

    

      

        
        
Step 1
          
Install the CirclePay Grok Bot template, or use a link

          
Use the template in Grok Bot, or tap one of the Open in your agent options for Grok, ChatGPT or Claude. Grok Bot has no public prompt link, so copy the prompt and paste it into the template.

      

      

        
        
Step 2
          
Connect PayBox only when you want to pay out

          
Building the card needs no wallet. Connect PayBox later, and only if you decide to reward your circle.

      

      

        
        
Step 3
          
Confirm each payout

          
You see who gets what first. Every payout needs your explicit yes, with no exceptions and nothing automatic.
